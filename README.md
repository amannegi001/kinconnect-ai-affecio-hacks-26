# KinConnect AI

**Find your people. Build your circle.**

KinConnect AI is an AI-powered social discovery platform that helps people find friends, communities, and activity partners through shared interests, personal goals, and compatible preferences.

## Problem Statement

**Affecio Hacks '26 — Problem Statement 03: Find Your People**

People often struggle to discover friends, communities, and activity partners beyond their existing social circles.

## Our Solution

KinConnect AI aims to make social discovery more meaningful by using AI to understand what people are looking for, recommend compatible connections, explain shared interests, and suggest activities people can enjoy together.

## Planned Features

- **Natural-Language Onboarding:** Understand users' interests, goals, and social preferences.
- **AI-Powered Matching:** Recommend compatible people based on shared interests and preferences.
- **Explainable Recommendations:** Show why people may be a good match.
- **Group Discovery:** Recommend small groups around shared interests and goals.
- **Activity Suggestions:** Suggest activities that compatible people can enjoy together.
- **Activity Participation:** Allow users to express interest in and join suggested activities.

## Planned Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js (React) with TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Next.js Route Handlers
- **Database:** MongoDB
- **AI Integration:** Large Language Model API
- **Deployment:** Vercel
